Frequently asked questions

Any questions?

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Lessons, images, Italian audio and audio classes are included in the app and work offline. Internet is only used for buying the full version through Bazaar and showing ads in the free version.

Can I start from scratch?

Yes. The app starts with the Italian alphabet and your first words, with all explanations in Persian. If you already know some Italian, take the placement test to start at the right level.

Is the full version a subscription?

No. Buy once through Cafe Bazaar and keep it forever. If you change phones, use Restore Purchase with the same Bazaar account.

Where is my progress stored?

Only on your phone. You can create a backup from the More section and restore it on a new phone.

Which devices are supported?

Android phones and tablets running Android 5.1 or later.
